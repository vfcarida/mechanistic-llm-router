# Architectural Roadmap & Maturity Governance

This document defines the architectural vision, maturity governance model, and capability roadmap for **Mechanistic LLM Router** (`mechanistic-llm-router`).

---

## 1. Vision & Architectural North Star

Enterprise and research AI deployments face an acute trade-off between **inference unit economics** and **epistemic reliability**. Standard black-box LLM routers rely on surface lexical features or opaque external reward models without distribution-free failure guarantees or causal feature validation.

**Mechanistic LLM Router** establishes a white-box, auditable alternative anchored on four scientific pillars:

1. **Encoder-Target Decoupling:** Decouple lightweight prefill representation extraction ($135\text{M}$–$1\text{B}$ open-weight models or shared encoder trunks) from downstream generation across heterogeneous model pools (`SLM-BERTau-Local`, `LLM-Mid-Tier`, `LLM-Frontier-Oracle`).
2. **Causal Activation Probing & Sparse Autoencoder (SAE) Circuit Analysis:** Extract internal residual-stream directions, Effective Dimensionality ($d_{\text{eff}}$), Fisher discriminant separability ($J$), and Top-K SAE sparse feature activations (`SAEEngine`), verifying directional necessity via counterfactual directional ablation (`CausalAblationHarness`).
3. **Finite-Sample Conformal Risk Control (CRC):** Calibrate multi-tier cascade thresholds ($\lambda_1, \lambda_2$) with distribution-free finite-sample risk guarantees ($\mathbb{E}[L(\lambda)] \le \alpha$) and binary Shannon entropy epistemic uncertainty abstention (`ConformalRiskController`).
4. **Zero-Leakage Pareto Evaluation:** Enforce strict prompt-disjoint train/dev/test splits, paired bootstrap 95% confidence intervals, and convex-hull Pareto AUC evaluation before promoting any routing policy to production.

---

## 2. Maturity Tiers & Verification Governance

Every subsystem in `mechanistic-llm-router` is governed by an explicit, objective maturity taxonomy. A component may only graduate to the next tier when all verification gates are satisfied in CI:

| Maturity Tier | Engineering & Verification Requirements |
|---|---|
| **`Stable`** | Complete implementation in `src/mechanistic_router/`, strict `mypy` type safety, $\ge 90\%$ automated test coverage (including edge cases and leakage guards), deterministic seeding, and synchronized reference documentation. |
| **`Beta`** | Functional implementation in `src/mechanistic_router/` with passing unit and integration tests on synthetic or cached fixtures; undergoing real-world corpus validation (e.g., full RouterBench evaluation). |
| **`Experimental`** | Research spike or prototype validated on controlled benchmarks with documented methodology and causal/statistical falsification gates (`Go` / `No-Go`). |
| **`Planned`** | Architectural design specified for future milestones; not yet shipped in the runtime package. |
